Plans and payment

Free

Every feature is on the free plan, with no credit card. A free workspace can store 50 MB, uploads included, and write 50,000 database rows in any 24 hours. Past either limit, changes are refused, but reading, signing in, exporting and upgrading keep working.

When all free workspaces together reach the service's overall limits, free workspaces may stop accepting changes, and sign-ups may pause, until use falls again.

Pro

Pro raises the limits to 5 GB of storage and 200,000 database rows written in any 24 hours. It costs $8 a month, billed monthly through Stripe. Until Pro is on sale, the app and the sign-up page say it's coming soon.

  • An admin subscribes in Settings › Plan, and pays on Stripe's checkout page.
  • You can cancel any time under Manage billing. Cancelling stops future charges, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • After that, the workspace goes back to the free plan. Nothing is deleted, but if it's over the free limits it can only shrink until it fits.
  • Stripe's terms apply to payments.

We may change prices or limits. We'll post a notice on dumbslab.com first, and a new price applies to a subscription only from its next billing period.
